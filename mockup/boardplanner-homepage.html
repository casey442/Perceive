The four parts of every meeting
01

The packet

Goes out days early. Templates hold the shape, and forwarded emails file themselves onto the agenda. When an item changes Monday night, you swap one page and the packet stays whole.

02

The minutes

Start from the agenda and follow the meeting item by item: motions, votes, roll calls. Each entry ties back to the posted notice, and the minute book runs complete back to 1998.

03

The board

Carries Tuesday's packet, their own notes, and the history behind item 7.C on the phone in their pocket. The binder stays on the shelf.

04

The public

Reads the same record you keep: agendas and approved minutes, posted inside your state's notice window and searchable by anyone. Every summary cites the page it came from.
